01 / THE PERSON

A LITTLE CONTEXT. A LOT LEFT TO EXPLORE.

I’m Abhi.
I like getting
to the inside.

Inside a device. Inside an idea.
Inside the question behind the question.

I’m studying Computer Science at BVRIT Narsapur, but my curiosity rarely ends at a screen. I’m interested in what happens when code meets circuits, when an idea becomes a physical thing, and when that thing becomes useful to someone.

I enjoy understanding how the pieces fit: the electronics, the intelligence, the design, and the business behind it. I’m still learning. Following those connections is how I find my next direction.

And when I close the laptop? A table tennis match, a workout, or a different view through a camera.
